01
DV360 · Programmatic Ads
Mitsubishi Motors
Precise area targeting for regional exhibitions — without losing CTR.
Objective

Implement precise area targeting for each exhibition without decreasing CTR on the RMB Ads campaigns.

Problem

The DSP tools at the time couldn't do pinpoint area targeting outside Jakarta, making the approach look unfeasible.

Solution

Used DV360 location targeting by generating a custom Deal ID and running the campaign independently via the Deal ID tag.

1.20% Campaign held a stable CTR above 1% while hitting the precise regional targeting goal.
